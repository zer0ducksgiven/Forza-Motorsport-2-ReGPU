About FM4:ReGPU

A native PC port of Forza Motorsport 4 (Xbox 360). Nothing is emulated: the game's PowerPC code is statically recompiled to x64, it runs on our own implementation of the Xbox 360 kernel, and a native Direct3D 12 renderer (ReGPU) replaces the console's GPU.

No game data is included: it plays from your own copy of the disc.

Built on FM2:ReGPU, the Forza Motorsport 2 port, and shares its kernel, renderer and launcher.
